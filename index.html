Step 1

🥔 That's it. You can share your screen.

  • Wiggle the mouse so the buttons come back
  • Click the green Share Screen button
  • Pick your screen, then click Share
  • Green border = it's working. Stop Share when you're done.
If it still won't work, that's not on you. Some school laptops block it until someone flips a setting. Tell me on our next call and we'll fix it together — don't spend your whole morning fighting it.
