Step 1

🥔 That's it \u2014 both ways.

  • Wiggle the mouse so the buttons come back
  • Click the green Share Screen button
  • Whole screen: pick Screen → click Share. Always works.
  • Just TimeBack: open TimeBack first, then pick it in the list → click Share
  • Green border = it's working. Stop Share when you're done.
